Master STEP contains every part separated on a 100 mm grid. STL uses cloud preview tessellation, not printer G-code. All 52 reloaded meshes passed watertightness, consistent winding and positive-volume checks. See mesh-validation.json for sizes and hashes.
PRINT: PETG prototype assumption, 0.4 mm nozzle / 0.2 mm layer. Select orientation and supports in your slicer; bed-zeroed is not optimal orientation. Lid posts, cap threads, side features and speaker retainer need review. Tiny light inserts and 0.6 mm tabs may require finer nozzle/resin. Use translucent material for light inserts. Trial print sliders and thread fit before full assembly.
ASSEMBLY: install captive controls from inside before PCB; motor in rear cradle; speaker in top seat with retainer; PCB on posts; insulating tray and pull-release battery adhesive; route leads without pinching; light inserts/lid and rear screws; privacy cap/backer. Confirm hardware sizes and sequence by physical trial.
LIMITATIONS: battery/PCM/lead dimensions, component heights, camera/USB datums and SW2 height/2 mm throw are provisional. CAD solids and sampled slider travel passed, not actual fit, forces, fatigue, acoustics or thermal behavior. PCB remains unrouted with DRC errors. Do not treat as production-ready.
