# Integrated neck-cord loop

The rear shell includes a rounded top bail, not attached to the removable lid or magnetic backer. Default outer width 12 mm, projection above body 9 mm, thickness 3 mm, clear rounded opening 6 x 6 mm. The attachment overlaps the shell by 3 mm. It lies at PCB-frame Z=-9 to -6 mm, behind the provisional USB aperture at Z=2 to 6.5 mm. Cable/plug access still needs physical verification.

Use a breakaway neck-cord clasp. Printed loop strength, cord abrasion, snag release, and wearing comfort require prototype testing; this is not a load-rated attachment. Deburr/finish all cord-contact edges. Shell-floor-down orientation keeps the loop flat for printing. Existing backer tunnel is retained but is not the neck attachment.

The four per-part manifests share pendant.py and use its default bail dimensions unless overridden. Rebuild the assembly and rear-shell design after changing the loop. Previous STEP exports do not contain this feature.
