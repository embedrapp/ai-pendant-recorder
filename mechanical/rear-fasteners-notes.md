# Rear-entry enclosure fastening

Four front holes removed. Four rear counterbores, diameter 4.2 mm and depth 1.3 mm, align with existing PCB mounting holes. Base passages are diameter 2.2 mm. Front-cover posts end at PCB top, trapping the board against rear standoffs when assembled. Blind 1.6 mm pilot bores accept a provisional M2-class plastic thread-forming screw; do not assume a tapped metric thread.

Candidate screw: 12 mm under-head length, head <=4.0 mm diameter and <=1.2 mm height. At current parameters the bearing plane is Z-4.2, tip Z7.8 and pilot entrance Z1.62: nominal engagement 6.18 mm. Blind pilot end Z8.92 leaves 1.12 mm tip allowance. Front exterior stays unpierced. Exact screw procurement, pilot size, post strength and torque require a trial print; 3.6 mm post OD was retained to avoid the battery. No heat-set inserts are modeled.

Rear driver access is outside the center clothing backer. Remove four rear screws to release front cover, then service tray/PCB. Do not overtighten against FR4 or battery. Existing assumed component-envelope checks remain in the generator; no PCB changes.
