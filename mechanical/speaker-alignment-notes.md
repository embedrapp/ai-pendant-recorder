# Upper-front speaker alignment

Speaker seat, removable clamp and 9 x 5 dot grid now share X=camera_x, Y=22 mm in PCB frame. Positive Y is the neck/cord end. This removes the old arbitrary X4/Y4 offset and aligns the grille with the camera and cord centerline at default parameters. Forty-five 1 mm holes on 1.7 mm pitch are unchanged; grille spans 14.6 x 7.8 mm, within the 16.6 x 9.6 mm speaker seat opening.

Motor cradle moves from (14,15) to (14,5) to clear the relocated speaker clamp. No PCB or electrical changes. The front-facing grille is higher and nearer the wearer; it is not a directional acoustic beam or an upward-facing outlet. Acoustic level, leakage and intelligibility at the ears need a worn test. A top-facing outlet would require a separate duct/antenna-space redesign.

CAD assertions check speaker envelope against shell, motor cradle and camera cap, clamp against motor cradle, centerline alignment and upper-wall allowance. Speaker 16.6 x 9.6 x 3.5 mm envelope remains provisional. Lead length, actual speaker thickness, gasket, antenna clearance and audio performance still need physical verification. Existing overall-fit and unrouted-PCB limitations remain.
